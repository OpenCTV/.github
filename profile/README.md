# OpenCTV

Open source tooling for diagnosing connected TV advertising delivery.

Streaming ad breaks fail in the seams between systems: the ad decision,
the manifest, the stitcher, the player, and the measurement layer each
hold a partial account of what happened. OpenCTV builds tools that
reconstruct those accounts and identify where they stop agreeing.

Maintained by Prashant Chaudhary. Not affiliated with any standards
organization.
